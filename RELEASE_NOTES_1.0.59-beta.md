**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.59 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 59. The last release was 1.0.58 beta.

**Panels and Tabs**

- Panel tabs have a new look
- Panels lie over the picture, so opening a panel no longer zooms the picture out or shrinks it.
- Menus open under their names; the selected menu item is solid blue.
- The status bar is simplified, with items no taller than 22 pixels.

**Themes**

- Three separate themes: Krimble Blue-Gray (the default), Krimble Bevel (raised and recessed edges, with the thickest lines) and Krimble Painted.

**Layers Panel**

- A search box for layer names.
- Mask, adjustment layer and fx buttons in the top row.
- A type badge on every layer row, 48 pixel thumbnails by default, a tidier header grid, and symbol-only buttons under the list.
- The blending mode box is at the far right and opens on a long press.

**Tools**

- Healing Brush and Polygon and Star tools.
- Long press flyouts for every toolbox group: Marquee, Lasso, Wand, Shapes, Fill (Gradient first) and more.
- The Pan tool is selected when Krimble starts, and opening a file keeps the tool you had selected.
- Auto Crop in the Image menu.

**Color and Pre-press**

- Color Lab keeps its size when the panel gets narrower; a color goes into the recent colors only after you commit it to the foreground.
- Custom CMYK now makes an ICC output profile, so Convert Image Color Space and soft proofing can use your own ink and paper settings.

**Channels Panel**

- Tap a channel to show only that channel; All shows every channel again.
- To selection (or Ctrl+tap) loads the selected channel as a selection.

**Known issues**

- The Healing Brush icon is a placeholder.
