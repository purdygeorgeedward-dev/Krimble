**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.74 BETA**

**Transform**

- Each Transform mode is its own button: Scale (keeps proportions, the default), Stretch (non-uniform scale), Rotate, Shear, Perspective, Warp, Cage, Liquify and Mesh. They are in Tool Options and in the Transform button's long-press menu, with the same icons in both places.
- The Transform button in the toolbox shows the icon of the mode in use. Scale, Stretch and Shear switch without resetting the picture, and the last one chosen stays for the next transform.
- The mode buttons in Tool Options are bigger.
- Tool Options shows the tool and the mode on one line, for example "Transform – Shear". The tool name no longer runs into the panel's grip.
- New icons for Stretch and Shear; a new, clearer Rotate icon.
- No more "Free" in names: Free Transform is Scale, Free Rotate is Rotate (also in Image > Rotate).

**Look**

- One Aqua look for push buttons, checked buttons, check boxes, slider fills and progress bars, darker than before, with a shaped shine and dark curved bottom corners.
- Selections in lists, menus and layers are a flat blue.
- Progress bars show their percentage in the middle.
- Panel tab rows: the space behind the tabs is dark, the selected tab matches its panel, other tabs are a little darker.
- New theme: **Krimble Light**, a light gray theme with the same blue selections and Aqua buttons.

**Toolbox**

- Default icon size 22.
- A fourth button background, **Dark gray**. White icons are only shown on dark backgrounds: with the Light gray or White background they are drawn in black.
- The eyedropper icon shows again; Undo is black in the Black icon style.
- Soften leads the Smudge group (Smudge and Sharpen in its long-press menu).
- A lip at the top of the toolbox, and fewer grip lines.

**Menus and windows**

- File > **A. I. Image gen** opens the free Image Donkey image generator in the browser. Save the picture there and open it in Krimble.
- Image > Image Rotation is now Image > **Rotate**.
- The File menu sits a little closer to the K.
- Quick Adjust shows the large Krimble logo.

**Gradient editor**

- A large preview box shows the gradient running top to bottom as you edit it.
- Steadier by touch: a new color stop is added only by a clean tap, a stop moves only on a deliberate drag, and the handles are easier to grab.

**Mixer**

- The mixing pad now paints with Krimble's real brush engine and your current brush, so mixing brushes really blend colors on it. Paint, Move, Sample, Clear and + Color.

**Faster start, smaller app**

- Five patterns come with the app instead of 114 (paper, dots, a diagonal hatch, a crosshatch and a grid). For Zip-A-Tone dots and lines, use the Screentone fill layer.
- Animation templates, storyboard templates and the YCbCr color profiles are no longer installed.
- The photorealistic toolbox icons are held back from the app; the Glow button background is gone.

**Staying open**

- The Back button no longer closes Krimble; it sends it to the background, so switching apps should no longer restart it.
