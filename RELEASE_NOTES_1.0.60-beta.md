**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.60 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 60. The last release was 1.0.58 beta.

**Panels and Tabs**

- A panel docked on its own now has a tab with its name. Drag the tab to tear the panel off.
- Color Lab names itself once: by its tab in a tab group, by its logo otherwise.
- The menu bar shows the orange K on top; menus no longer slide up over their own titles.
- Panels and the toolbox paint the theme's own background.

**Layers Panel**

- Layer rows are mid gray and the selected layer is lighter, with no glow.

**Toolbox**

- A docked toolbox expands to fit its icons instead of scrolling.
- The toolbox snaps flush to the screen edge instead of docking into a box.
- The Heal tool has its icon again.
- Full color tool pictures fill the button.
- Raised buttons have a four-tone bevel that follows the theme.

**Windows and Dialogs**

- QuickAdjust opens as a small panel, keeps its contents at normal size, and its bottom buttons stay in rows.
- Save As shows every file type when it opens.
- The Preferences keyboard icon is the new orange K keycap, shown at full size.
- The icons in the New dialog are a little bigger.
- New pictures open fitted and centered with a margin.

**Startup**

- Krimble starts faster: fewer fonts, patterns and brush bundles are installed.

**Known issues**

- Raised toolbox buttons and the dock tab drag are new and need testing on the phone.
- Submenus can still overlap their parent menu.
