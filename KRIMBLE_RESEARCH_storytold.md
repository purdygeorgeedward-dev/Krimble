# Research notes: github.com/storytold (ArtCraft, PhotoCraft and the other "Crafting Apps")

Read 2026-10-23 (Mountain Time) at George's request ("Scour the code and notes. Learn anything useful."). Public repositories only; nothing was copied into Krimble.

## What it is
- **ArtCraft** (60k followers, 68 repositories): "Open Source Tools and Models for Makers, Builders, and Creatives". Pure **Rust**, native desktop and web (macOS, Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, Web); **no Android**.
- **PhotoCraft** (`storytold/photocraft`, 71k stars): "an open-source, clean-room reimplementation of Adobe Photoshop" (layers, masks, adjustment layers, layer styles, type, vectors, brushes, real PSD files). Status: **early alpha**. The same family: LightCraft (Lightroom-like), VectorCraft, FilmCraft, EffectCraft (After Effects-like), DesignCraft, CADCraft, DeckCraft, SoundCraft, craft-libs, craft-launcher.
- **License:** MIT **or** Apache-2.0 (at your option); fonts, icons and images keep their own licenses (`ATTRIBUTION.md`); their brand assets may not be reused. Both licenses can go into Krimble (GPL) with attribution. The code is Rust: anything useful has to be **rewritten in C++** (write it from the idea, keep their notice where code is derived).
- Their method is "clean room": Photoshop was **measured as a black box** (synthetic and public-domain test images, results read back; none of its code or assets used), and the algorithms are from published papers. The documents in `docs/` record those measurements.

## What is useful for Krimble (mapped to the open items)
1. **Content-Aware Fill ("just a blur")** - `crates/algo/src/content_aware.rs` and `inpaint.rs` (read the `//!` notes at the top of both):
   - Photoshop's **sampling window**: a square centred on the selection's bounding box, side `4 * sqrt(max(w,50) * max(h,50))` (200 px at least; it slides inwards at the canvas edge); pixels outside it never change the result. An **Auto sampling area** leaves out regions of another brightness or texture; a **paintable sampling area** is the workspace's other mode.
   - The fill is **pieces copied pixel for pixel, 15 to 40 px across, joined by seams a few pixels wide**; colour adaptation None / Default / High / Very High (gain and bias per patch, after Darabi et al., *Image Melding*, 2012); rotation, scale and mirror adaptation by extending the window with transformed copies (Barnes et al. 2010).
   - Multi-scale EM (Wexler, Shechtman, Irani 2007) with PatchMatch (Barnes 2009). **The coarsest level starts by filling the hole ring by ring from the edge inwards, each pixel from the patch that best matches what is known around it, "rather than from a smooth membrane, which draws the fill towards flat areas".** Krimble's engine (`krimble_inpaint.cpp`, `rPeel`) starts the coarsest level from an **average of the surroundings**, which is the likely source of the blur on busy pictures: this is the first thing to change.
   - **Thin holes** (a selected wire or hair) are measured by their **thickness**, not their bounds; the pyramid stops where the hole is still about two patches thick.
2. **Auto Color** - `crates/engine/src/edit_cmds.rs`, function `auto_adjust`: per-channel levels with **0.1% clipping**; Auto Color then **neutralises the midtones** (each channel's median is moved onto the average of the three medians with a per-channel gamma); Auto Contrast uses the same black and white points for all channels. This is Photoshop's behaviour. It is **not** "sweeten the saturation without changing the tone" (George's wish, test list item 14): for that, keep the levels step very light (or off) and add a **vibrance-style** step (raise the chroma of the dull pixels most, keep lightness), with the midtone neutralising as an option.
3. **Indexed Color and palettes (George: "8-bit 256 color palettes")** - `crates/algo/src/quantize.rs`: palettes **exact, system (Mac, Windows), web (216), uniform, perceptual, selective, adaptive**, forced colours, and dithering **none, Floyd-Steinberg, ordered (Bayer 8), noise**; median cut (Heckbert 1982) with k-means refinement; also 1-bit Bitmap conversion (halftone screens). Maps to a Krimble "Image > Mode > Indexed Color..." that reduces the colours of a layer or picture.
4. **Dialog conventions** - `docs/ui-dialogs.md`: no dimmed backdrop; the canvas can be panned and zoomed under an open dialog; Tab moves only between text and number fields; Esc cancels; Enter confirms; Alt turns Cancel into Reset. A **Reset All Tools** confirmation exists (`crates/ui-egui/src/tool_reset.rs`): the Photoshop item that matches Krimble's "Reset Tool Options on Start" toggle (253rd entry).
5. **Photoshop parity lists** - `docs/target-app-parity.md`, `docs/gaps.md`, `docs/scorecard.md`, `docs/parity-checklist.md`, `docs/context-menu-parity.md`, `docs/ui-parity.md`: very detailed feature-by-feature lists of Photoshop with what is done, partial or missing. Good raw material for Krimble's own checklist (Image > Adjustments, Edit menu, Select menu, Type, Layer Styles).
6. **Type** - `crates/text/` and the parity notes: manual and optical **kerning**, variable-font axis sliders, **area type** (text boxes), Warp Text; `docs/user/troubleshooting/text-moves-when-editing.md`; `docs/font-resolution.md`.
7. **Layer styles for the effects pack** (Krimble's Phase 2 plan: Drop Shadow, Glow, Bevel/Emboss) - `crates/engine/src/layer_style.rs` and `layer_style/gradient_overlay.rs`.
8. **File formats** - `docs/kra.md` (Krita's own .kra), `docs/ora.md`, PSD/PSB notes in `book/src/formats/`.
9. **Other algorithm modules** in `crates/algo/src/` (78): `poisson` (seamless cloning), `seam` (seam carving), `matting`, `remove`, `retouch`, `liquify`, `puppet`, `warp`, `perspective`, `vanishing`, `oil`, `stylize`, `lens`, `denoise`, `tone`, `histogram`, `relight`, `hdr`, `panorama`. A map of what a Photoshop-class editor contains.
