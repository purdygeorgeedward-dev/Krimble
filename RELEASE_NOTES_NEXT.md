**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN THE NEXT BETA**

(Draft. Everything listed here up to Nov 22, 2026 went into 1.0.74; see RELEASE_NOTES_1.0.74-beta.md. Changes after 1.0.74 go below.)

**Toolbox**

- A fourth button background, **Dark gray**.
- White icons are only shown on dark backgrounds: with the Light gray or White background they are drawn in black.

**File menu**

- **A. I. Image gen** opens the free Image Donkey image generator in the browser. Save the picture there and open it in Krimble.

**Gradient editor**

- A large preview box shows the gradient running top to bottom as you edit it.
- Steadier by touch: a new color stop is added only by a clean tap, a stop moves only on a deliberate drag, and the handles are easier to grab.

**Mixer**

- The mixing pad now paints with Krimble's real brush engine and your current brush, so mixing brushes really blend colors on it. Paint, Move, Sample, Clear and + Color.

**Faster start**

- Five patterns come with the app instead of 114 (paper, dots, a diagonal hatch, a crosshatch and a grid). For Zip-A-Tone dots and lines, use the Screentone fill layer.

**Transform**

- Each Transform mode is its own button: Scale (keeps proportions, the default), Stretch, Rotate, Shear, Perspective, Warp, Cage, Liquify and Mesh, in Tool Options and in the Transform button's long-press menu. The toolbox button shows the mode in use.
- No more "Free" in names: Free Transform is Scale, Free Rotate is Rotate.

**Look**

- One Aqua look for buttons, check boxes, slider fills and progress bars.

**Changes after 1.0.74**

- The background keep-alive feature is now called **Don'tCrash**. Its notice reads "Don'tCrash: Keeps Krimble open in the background", and the first line of Help > Show Krimble Log... reports Don'tCrash's status.
- Buttons: rounder Aqua corners; the thin glistening edges are gone (buttons that are not selected are plain raised buttons).
- The selected tool's highlight fills the whole toolbox button.
- The Tool Options panel's title says Tool Options again; the tool (and the Transform mode) is named on the first line inside the panel.
- **Panels** is its own menu in the menu bar, before Window: every panel command at the top (Toggle Panels, Detach and Attach, Hide Right Panels, Hide All Panels, Stack Panels), then the list of panels.
- **Comic brushes:** HB Pencil, Blue Pencil (non-photo blue, sets its color when chosen), Technical Pen (one width at any pressure) and Brush Pen (tapered, pressure-sized).
