**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN THE NEXT BETA**

**Toolbox**

- **Photorealistic** icon style: photographic pictures for the tools, Quick Mask (a mask) and Screen Mode (a TV). Tools without a photo are drawn in the Classic style.
- Two more button backgrounds: **Dark gray**, and **Glow** (a dark tile lit with a soft teal glow, made to go with the Photorealistic icons).
- White icons are only shown on dark backgrounds: with the Light gray or White background they are drawn in black.

**File menu**

- **A. I. Image gen** opens the free Image Donkey image generator in the browser. Save the picture there and open it in Krimble.
