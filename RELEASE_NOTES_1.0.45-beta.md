**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.45 BETA**

**A new look**

- **New look all through the app:** long smooth gradients on the windows, panels and status bar, lighter panels with darker recessed lists for more contrast, white text, and rounder buttons.
- **Selected items glow:** the selected tool in the toolbox, tab, list entry and OK button have a bright cyan outline, and the selected tool has a soft glow. Check boxes show a real check mark.
- **Menu bar:** a small Krimble K at the left. The Settings menu is gone: Preferences is under Edit, Full Screen is under View > Screen Mode, Customize Toolbar is under Window.
- **Toolbox:** 32 tools with new 3D-style icons, in Full color or Silhouette (switch it in the toolbox menu). An **UNDO** button sits under the color wells: tap to undo, long press for Redo.
- **Messages** (the little notices that say something is done) now appear at the bottom left of the picture instead of over it.
- **Preferences:** your own pictures for the pages, and the whole keyboard on Keyboard Shortcuts.

**New and improved tools**

- **QuickAdjust** (first in the Image menu): Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Hue, Lightness and Vibrance on one screen, a Preview switch, and **Keep as layers**.
- **Color panel:** a new color picker (Color Studio) with a palette, and a **Mixer** panel with a mixing and brush test pad.
- **Transform:** Scale is uniform by default (the aspect lock is on for every new transform) and no longer rotates by accident. The Position, Rotate, Scale and Shear buttons in Tool Options choose what a drag does, and there is a Rotate mode button. In Scale mode, a touch outside the handles moves the selection.
- **Type tool:** every new text starts on its own layer, so two texts no longer move together.
- **Line tool:** a Thickness setting.
- **Brushes panel:** Size, Opacity and Flow above the presets, and the name of the current brush.
- **Layer > New > Star...**
- **Image > Batch Process** and **Krimble Script** (from 1.0.44) are unchanged.

**Panels and windows**

- **File > New:** OK and Cancel are always visible at the bottom right, and the description box is shorter.
- **Panels** dock only when you deliberately drag one to a side. **Window > Stack Panels** also stacks panels that are floating.
- **Layers panel:** more empty space above and in front of the Layer Type drop-down, so the top of the panel is easier to grab without opening it.
- **Save As:** the file type list opens taller. Several windows keep their labels beside their fields.

**DPaint panel** (Window > DPaint, new and first version)

- Tools in two columns with black pixel icons on gray bevelled buttons, UNDO, redo, CLR and select all.
- The color indicator: the background color as a box with the foreground color as a dot (tap the dot or the box to pick; long press swaps them).
- A palette of 256 colors in pages of 4 x 8 wells. **RANGE**, then the first and the last well, fills the wells between them with a blend. **EDIT** changes a well. **BACK** undoes the last change. A DPaint toolbar is also available in Window > Toolbars.

**Known issues in this beta**

- The menu bar shows two Layer menus (a menu edit is unfinished).
- Tearing off the toolbox can change it to one column and then to a horizontal bar.
- The Color panel can be wider than a small screen.
- Quick Adjust and some panels have not been tested on every screen size.

**Install:** download the APK from this page, allow installs from this source, and open it. Beta builds are signed with the Krimble release key.
