**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.61 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 61. The last release was 1.0.58 beta.

**Do not install this build.** This build was installed and tested; the faults are listed under Known issues at the end.

**Toolbox**

- The toolbox never docks and grows until no tool scrolls, color wells included.
- The raised icon highlight is dimmer.

**Panels and Tabs**

- Layers rows and panel titles reworked; the Color Lab logo is named.
- No white top lines on panels, no bevel on maximized image windows or on empty tool bars.
- Bevel shading is less intense.

**Menus**

- Menus have a black outline instead of bevels.
- The selected menu row is recessed instead of bright blue.
- The menu bar's top bevel line is softer.

**Sliders and Color Lab**

- Slider handles everywhere use the steel ball bearing picture, in a row tall enough not to clip it.
- Picking a recent color again no longer reorders the recent colors.

**Windows and Dialogs**

- QuickAdjust is compact: one line per row, narrower to start.
- In Preferences the highlight is drawn under the selected icon.
- Buttons are less rounded.
- The Donate button is Krimble orange.

**Themes**

- Krimble Painted: the scroll bar track is darker and has more contrast with the handle.

**Known issues**

This build was installed and tested.

- The menu title still lights up blue instead of being recessed, and the menu rows got the recess instead: the opposite of what was asked.
- The recent files list is still covered by the toolbox; the base screen needs to be darker.
- The selected tab still does not connect with its own panel.
- Color Lab has no logo.
- The Layers panel still has no title.
