**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.72 BETA**

(Written Nov 22, 2026, from the build log and the change log; this build's notes were not kept in the repository before.)

- The File menu sits a fixed distance from the orange K, with or without an image open; the K on image windows and tabs is pinned near the corner.
- The toolbox bevel matches the raised buttons; the selected tool is a flat blue with no glow.
- No gap between the panels and the image area.
- Canvas messages are shown on the status bar instead of in boxes over the picture.
- A keep-alive service to keep Krimble from being restarted in the background.
- Pictures open fitted to the window; the first New picture is the size of the screen.
- Color Lab docks again and holds up to 14 palette rows.
- More room between the eye and the check mark in Layers.
