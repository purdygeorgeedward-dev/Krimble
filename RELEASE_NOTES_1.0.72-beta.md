**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.72 BETA**

**Menu bar and image windows**

- The orange K no longer sits on top of File: File starts 90 pixels after the K, with or without an image open.
- The K in image window title bars and image tabs is pinned 17 pixels from the corner.

**Toolbox and panels**

- The toolbox bevel is drawn like the raised buttons, with the highlight and shadow the same thickness.
- The selected tool is a flat blue square with no glow.
- No gray stripe between the panels and the image area.
- Layers: a gap between the eye and the check box, so tapping the eye no longer toggles the check.

**Color Lab**

- Color Lab docks again when dragged to the edge of the window.
- The palette grows up to 14 rows of 19 colors when Color Lab is made taller.

**Images**

- Images open fitted to the image area.
- The first New image of each session is the size of the screen.
- Zoom, rotation and other canvas messages show on the status bar instead of in pop-up boxes.

**Stability**

- Krimble stays running while you switch to other apps; a quiet "Krimble is open" notification shows while it is open.

**Known issues**

- The menus squeeze when Fill opens its submenu.
- A magenta box shows around a selection while it is moved.
- Aqua outlines and highlights instead of the selection blue.
- Grab lines and the Layers title do not match the mockup.
- Krita's animation tools (Timeline, Onion Skins) are not included.

Found in testing this build:

- The Recent Images area was too small, with tiny scroll bars.
- Save As was still far too wide; it should be taller, not wider.
- There were no grip lines on the black part of the Color Lab banner.
