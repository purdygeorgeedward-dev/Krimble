# Krimble v1.0.58-beta

**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.58 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 58. The last release was 1.0.57 beta.

**SmartCompression (saving a JPEG smaller where it does not show)**

- The JPEG save window has a **SmartCompression** tab, and the SmartCompression panel has a live preview with the original and compressed sizes.
- **Protection:** Smart (the program finds the detail to protect), Selection, or None, with a **Protection Sensitivity** setting, and a **Whole Picture** or **Regions** mode with rectangle, freehand, invert and all tools. Choosing Regions starts from the Smart Selection.
- **Extra Color, Grayscale and Progressive** options; gray pictures are saved as one channel.
- The panel was redrawn after a mockup: header band with the logo, a slider and number box for JPEG Quality and for Smart Compression, and a wide Save JPEG button.

**Toolbox and panels**

- The toolbox starts docked at the left with two columns, and does not start floating. A floating toolbox no longer turns horizontal by itself.
- The flyout icons are the toolbox's size.
- The Hexagon Selection tool (snaps to the hex grid) is in the Marquee flyout.
- The Color Lab's color wells have no outline and no glow.

**Windows and menus**

- The New window's column and icons are smaller.
- The Preferences keyboard page fills its column; the selection is bright blue again.
- The menu bar has its lines at the start, and the theme is applied before the window appears. The K shows in the corner.

**Known issues**

- The SmartCompression panel is new in this build and has not been used on a phone for long: report anything odd in its preview or protected area.
