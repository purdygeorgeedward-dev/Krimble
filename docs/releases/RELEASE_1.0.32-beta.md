**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.32 BETA**
- Image windows: floating windows are the default again. The first item in the Window menu switches between Tabs and Windows in one tap. A second switch, Show Contents While Dragging, shows the picture while you move a window.

- Toolbox: the foreground and background color wells and the screen mode button now sit right under the last tool and scroll with the tools.

- Toolbox: the drag bar at the top shows light grip lines, so it's clear it can be grabbed.

- Toolbox: the floating toolbox lost its empty margin, stripe and dots on the right and bottom edges.

- Toolbox: after a screen rotation or a layout restore, the toolbox goes back to the number of columns you chose.

- The strips between the toolbox, canvas and panels keep their original look, with a wider area to grab.

- Save As and the format picker now fit the screen.

- New splash image. The version shown on launch matches the release.

**KNOWN ISSUES**
- The app may restart when you switch away from it and come back.

- Custom toolbars are rough. A toolbar can get stuck vertical, and its tools don't arrange themselves into columns.

- After a screen rotation the Window menu can stay open.

- The close button on a floating image window is not drawn as an X.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
