**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.31 BETA**
- Dock menu: long-press a panel's title bar, or the toolbox, to dock it left, right, top or bottom, or to undock it. No dragging needed.
- Toolbox: starts at 2 columns, and rotating the screen no longer changes the column count. Drag it wider than 4 columns if you like. The foreground and **background** color wells grow with it. The tools are one continuous block, with no groups.
- Easier to grab: the strips between the toolbox, canvas and panels are wider on touch screens.
- Drag the strip beside the right panels all the way to the screen edge to shut them. Window > Show Right Panels brings them back.
- Window menu: panel controls, toolbars, themes, styles and language are all there, and the View menu is lighter. Interface Scale... sits next to Zoom.
- Menus: clearer dividers, and no keyboard shortcut text, since there is no keyboard.
- New splash image. The version shown on launch now matches the release.

**KNOWN ISSUES**
- In portrait, the toolbox can fall back to one column and is harder to resize. Landscape is fine.
- In portrait, the Save As window can be bigger than the screen. A two-finger drag moves it.
- The app may restart when you switch away from it and come back.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
