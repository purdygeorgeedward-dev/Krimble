**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.63 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 63. The last stable release was 1.0.58 beta. You can try the more recent one, but I don't recommend it.

**Do not install this build.** Tested; the faults are listed under Known issues at the end.

**Themes**

- Breeze Dark and Breeze Light no longer get blue bevel lines or a dark menu bar; bevel tones come from each theme's own colors.
- An old saved Krita theme name loads Krimble Blue-Gray.
- Switching themes is faster.

**Menus**

- Menus have no outline and open at the bottom of the menu bar.
- The Window menu has a separator before the language entry.
- The message about missing resource bundles is gone.

**Panels and Tabs**

- A selected tab has its panel's color, with no line between them.
- Color Lab shows one title in a tab group.
- Layers rows use warm grays; the black bars beside the Layers buttons are gone.
- The toolbox docking code is back to its 1.0.58 state.

**QuickAdjust**

- Smaller slider handles, wider number boxes, and a smaller Preview switch in the bottom row.

**Known issues**

George installed this build and tested it.

- The toolbox did not dock normally; docking by menu or by dragging gave unpredictable results, and Dock Right could put it at the top.
- The docked toolbox was cut off halfway down and had to be scrolled.
- The right dock had no highlight line on its left side.
- The Window menu did not work right; in landscape the Toolbars entry was off the screen.
- The K started too close to the menus and corrected itself later.
- Color Lab was drawn at giant size in a wide panel.
- In Preferences the highlight still tinted the selected icon.
- The Retouch flyout (stamp icon) listed tools without names.
- In the Raised toolbox style three buttons (Stamp, Gradient, UNDO) had no raised box.
