**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.37 BETA**

- **New splash screen** (a night scene), and a rebuilt **About Krimble** page: the whole picture, the version under it, and four links in one column.

- **A new Type tool.** Tap the canvas and a text window opens: type your text, pick the font, size, bold, italic, alignment and color, then OK. The text goes on the picture as vector text. The old Type tool, its menu and its toolbar are gone. The default text size is bigger. (To change the words of existing text, make new text for now. Move, turn and scale it with the Move and Transform tools.)

- **Hex maps.** View > Show > Hex draws a hexagon pattern over the canvas: pinch to scale the hexes, tap to drop, and say yes to make it a layer. Layer > New > Hex Pattern... makes a transparent vector layer with the size, line thickness and line color you choose, pointy-top or flat-top. Made for game and map makers.

- **Free Rotate.** A new toolbox button, and Image > Image Rotation > Free Rotate. It rotates the layer with two fingers (see Known Issues).

- **Panels join as tabs again** when you drop one on another. Docked panels, floating panels, toolbars and menus all have one-pixel light and dark edges. Floating panels have blue-gray title bars and bigger buttons. A detached panel is less eager to re-dock. The toolbox docks at the edge instead of bouncing, and it is never taller than its contents. The side column starts narrower. The resize edges are bigger.

- **Layers panel:** its contents are moved away from the left edge, and the strip is headed "Layer Type".

- **Smudge now smudges** (it no longer adds color). Your own brush comes back after Smudge, Soften, Dodge and Burn.

- **File > Take Photo...** works: it copies the picture before opening it, and shows the reason if something goes wrong.

- **Brushes** is hidden when you launch, and again when your first image opens.

- **Smaller windows:** the Healing Brush and other wait windows, Help > Presets... (without its details pane), Image > Mode > Index Color, and the crop tool options, which now line up in one column.

- **Bigger things to touch:** the up and down arrows of number boxes, the icons on the Preferences list, a bigger Free Rotate icon. The crop tool's corner boxes and the text tool's circles are smaller.

- **The memory limit** starts at 25% of the phone's memory (it was 50%), to make Android less likely to close Krimble in the background.

- **Also:** the Photo Edit toolbar lost its two crop buttons, a Canvas Only black X leaves that mode, Swatches Palette has its new title, and Send Feedback is gone from Help.

**KNOWN ISSUES (found in testing)**

- Free Rotate: two fingers do not rotate yet; it behaves like the Transform tool and scales.
- The icons on the File > New list are still drawn small.
- The JPG export options window is much wider than the screen, and the Content-Aware and Healing Brush report windows are still very wide.
- A JPG may be proposed with the .jpeg extension.
- The toolbox has no edge lines.
- Brushes can still show at launch.
- The Layers panel's bottom buttons wrap into two rows, and tabbed panels show their title twice.
- A panel dropped at the top or bottom edge does not always dock; the Tool Options panel sometimes bounces.
- Switching to another app can restart Krimble.
- Dodge and Burn paint like black or white crayons.
- The vertex circles of the Polygon select tool are too big.
- Content-Aware Fill gives poor results.
- The new Type tool and hex maps are brand new and lightly tested.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
