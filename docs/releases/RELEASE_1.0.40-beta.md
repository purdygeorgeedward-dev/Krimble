**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.40 BETA**

- **New splash screens:** a new main splash and a new holiday splash (shown Dec 1 to 26), both made in Krimble.
- **Free Rotate shows the picture turning** under your finger as you drag, not just the angle.
- **Select > Similar** (Photoshop-style) is added to the Select menu.
- **Content-Aware Fill has new engines.** Edit > Fill > Content-Aware... copies real pixels from the best matching patches (turned, resized and mirrored copies, with colour adaptation), instead of blurring. The Smart Patch (healing brush) tool has a **Fill method** choice in its Tool Options: Sharp, Smooth, or Classic.
- **Crop is final:** a tap after a crop no longer undoes it. The crop handles are smaller.
- **Selection tools:** in Add (and Subtract, Intersect) mode, a stroke near the selection's edge now adds instead of moving the selection.
- **Transform tool:** switching mode (Free, Perspective, Warp, Cage, Liquify, Mesh) now applies the work done so far instead of throwing it away. The mode buttons are bigger and the chosen one is coloured.
- **Toolbox:** it snaps to an edge less eagerly, no longer leaves empty space when docked at the top, and reshapes when switched to Horizontal while floating.
- **File > New:** smaller template pictures and a narrower column.
- **Fonts:** your bundled fonts (the five Purdy Design fonts and 52 open-license families) are now found on Android.
- **Type tool:** a title at the top of its window, and colour emoji fonts as fallbacks.

**KNOWN ISSUES**

- **Content-Aware Fill may do nothing** on large areas (it may run out of memory). Fixes are made for the next version.
- **Emoji do not show** in the Type tool yet. The font list still shows the phone's own Noto and Samsung fonts.
- The panel resize handles are invisible, and the Hand tool can move the layer instead of the view.
- The status bar's zoom slider is easy to hit by accident.
- Dodge and Burn only paint black or white. After Smudge, the Brush, Line and Circle tools keep the Smudge brush.
- The transform mode buttons are too big. Resizing the Preferences window from its corner bounces it back.
- The Type tool makes one line of text only (no text boxes), and has no kerning controls.

Fixes for these are already made for the next version.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
