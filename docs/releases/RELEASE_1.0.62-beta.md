**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.62 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 62. The last stable release was 1.0.58 beta. You can try the more recent one, but I don't recommend it.

**Do not install this build.** Tested: the faults are listed under Known issues at the end.

**Themes**

- New theme Krimble Dark Gray: flat, warm grays, no bevels, Krimble orange selection, square corners.
- The Krita themes and Krimble Blue are removed; no theme has Krita in its name or file.
- The selected menu bar title is recessed and menu rows light up.

**Startup and Size**

- Only five brush presets are installed; the brush bundles are gone.
- The nine biggest font families are removed (Montserrat stays).

**Welcome Screen**

- The welcome page sits right of the toolbox, the base screen is darker, and the recent files area is darker still.
- Menus start further right of the orange K.

**Toolbox**

- The toolbox has bevel edges on all four sides with clearer tones.

**Panels and Tabs**

- The tab customizations are removed.

**Known issues**

George installed this build and tested it.

- After switching themes the theme would not change again; Krimble Blue-Gray would not load (an old saved Krita theme name), and switching was slow.
- The File menu was still too close to the K.
- Color Lab showed three titles; panel titles were doubled and looked wrong.
- Panels were twitchy and would not dock; Layers would not dock with tabs, and the right dock vanished once when the Layers tab was tapped.
- After loading an image the Layers panel was squashed and its contents overlapped.
- A message said there were no resource bundles.
- Breeze Dark had blue bevel lines and Breeze Light had a dark menu bar.
- Black bars beside the add and properties buttons at the bottom of the Layers panel.
- In Preferences the highlight still tinted the selected icon.
- Menus opened a little too high, over the bottom of the menu bar.
- QuickAdjust's ball bearings were too big, its numbers had no room, and the Preview switch was too big.
