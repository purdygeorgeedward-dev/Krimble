**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.75 BETA**

**Menus and panels**

- **Panels** is its own menu in the menu bar, just before Window. Every panel command is in it: the panel commands at the top (Show Panels, Detach Panel, Attach Panel, Show Right Panels, Hide All Panels, Stack Panels), then a divider, then the list of panels.
- Closed panels no longer come back by themselves (Layers kept reopening when images were opened).
- Each phone direction keeps its own panel arrangement, as you last left it.
- Panel grips have six lines; the toolbox grip is a raised bevel with five lines.
- Tool Options: the tool's name no longer sits under the grip. When Tool Options shares a tab group, the tool's name is in the title row.
- Snap to Assistants: the Magnetism, Snap to Single Line and Snap Eraser rows always show (greyed out until the box is checked), so the panel no longer jumps.

**Brushes**

- New comic and inking brushes: **HB Pencil**, **Blue Pencil** (non-photo blue), **Technical Pen**, **Brush Pen** and an **Art Marker** for flat, even color.

**Tools and images**

- New images keep the tool you chose last, instead of switching to another tool.
- Images opening very small: they are fitted to the window again while it settles.
- Open images come back after Krimble restarts.
- Edit > Free Transform is now **Transform Scale**; the Transform tool's Free mode is **Scale**.

**Toolbox**

- New icons for **Quick Mask** and **Screen Mode**.
- The selected-tool highlight is a little smaller.

**Fonts**

- Liberation Serif, Liberation Sans and Liberation Mono are included, so files that ask for Times New Roman, Arial/Helvetica or Courier get matching letter widths.

**Staying open**

- Once per install, Krimble asks Android to let it run in the background without battery limits, so it is less likely to be closed while you switch apps.

**Other**

- Quick Adjust: the logo is smaller.
- The About page and the README describe Krimble as open source software.

This build was installed and tested.

**Known issues**

- Installing over the previous version can fail with "App not installed" when the phone is low on storage; freeing space fixes it.
- Pressed toggle buttons turn gray instead of blue, and the Aqua buttons look faded.
- Every menu has a light outline and extra space inside; a thin line separates an open menu from its title.
- Quick Adjust: the window became narrow along with the smaller logo.
- The toolbox grip has a bright white line along its top and left edges.
- Menus that spread into several columns (Panels) have no dividers between columns.
- In portrait, the window buttons overlap the menu titles and sit too far from the corner.
- Color Lab: an empty row above its logo band, and its content is pushed right and cut off.
- Focused boxes and drop-downs have a shaded blue edge that looks broken at the rounded corners.
- The strip for dragging the toolbox wider cannot be grabbed.
- The Quick Mask and Screen Mode icons are too big and come too close to the toolbox edges.
