**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.33 BETA**

- **Share:** File > Share as JPG or Share as PNG opens Android's share sheet (messages, email, social apps, cloud storage). The picture is also saved in Pictures/Krimble.

- **Help menu:** Where is...? (search every menu and open the entry), Send Feedback (a prefilled GitHub issue), and QA Overlay (live window and panel sizes for screenshots).

- **Window menu:** Hide All Panels, Layout Presets (Quick Edit, Retouch, Illustration, Design, Focus), a Tabs / Floating Windows switch, and Show Contents While Dragging. Floating windows are the default.

- **Toolbars:** stacked one per row, Reset Toolbars, and ten new toolbars (History, Selection, Layers, View, Retouch, Photo Edit, Transform, Quick Adjust, Save and Export, Filters), all hidden until you turn them on in Window > Toolbars.

- **Toolbox:** two columns by default, the Columns choice works floating or docked, one continuous block of tools, light grip lines on its drag bar, and the color wells sit right under the tools.

- **Panel edges:** a thin invisible strip with a small touch handle in the middle.

- **New Document:** 13 new size presets (social media, phone wallpaper, album cover, business card, postcard), colored section icons, a thinner left list.

- **Dodge and Burn:** now a slow, cumulative lighten and darken.

- **Type tool:** the keyboard comes back when you tap text to edit it again.

- **Dialogs:** two-finger drag to move any dialog, a corner grip to resize, Save As fits the screen, long text wraps.

- **Menus:** no keyboard shortcut column (a touch screen has no keyboard), clear 1 pixel dividers, real X, minimize and maximize buttons on windows.

- **Layers panel:** icons are a little smaller so the panel can be narrower.

- **New icon and splash,** and a larger brush icon.

**KNOWN ISSUES**

- **Cheat Sheet (Help menu) crashes when you pick a line.** Avoid it. It is removed in the next build.

- **Mirror Layout (Window menu) corrupts the display.** Avoid it. It is removed in the next build.

- Some windows (Preferences, Brightness/Contrast, the JPEG export window) are wider than the screen in portrait.

- Custom toolbars are rough: a toolbar can get stuck vertical, and its tools don't arrange into columns.

- After a screen rotation the Window menu can stay open.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
