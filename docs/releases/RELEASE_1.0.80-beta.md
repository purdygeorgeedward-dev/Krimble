**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.80 BETA**

**Opening images**

- Images open the way Krita opens them, fitted to the view. The extra zoom after opening and the other open-time fixes from 1.0.74 to 1.0.79 are gone.

- Zoom and rotation messages pop up over the picture again instead of in the status bar.

- The gap between the docked panels and the picture area is back to its normal size.

- The keep-alive service and its notification are gone.

- These changes target the bug where an opened image showed blurry and upside down. Testing older builds showed it first appeared in 1.0.72.

**Toolbox**

- The highlight along the top of the toolbox is thin (3 pixels).

- New icon sizes: 18 x 18 and 20 x 20.
In a one-column toolbox, the Quick Mask and Screen Mode buttons stack, so the toolbox stays one icon wide.

**New File dialog**

- Image Size and Color go side by side or -stack on their own, depending on the screen.

- One button switches between portrait and landscape.

- The dialog refits when the phone is turned.

**Look**

- Scrollbars have a raised grip, with ridges in the middle, in every theme.

- In the Painted theme the panel tabs match the panel color.

- A new ball bearing on the sliders, also in Color Lab.

- The About Krimble window shows a bigger splash.

**Cyber theme (new)**

- A flat red menu bar. (Under revision.)

- See-through menus with an angled corner and a cyan stripe, with solid cyan selections.

- Futuristic fonts throughout (Orbitron and Rajdhani).

- A black glass toolbox with a soft blue glow; the selected tool lights a blue LED.

- Dark buttons with a cut corner and a blue LED that brightens when pressed.

- Angled panel tabs; the selected tab is solid cyan.

-Dialogs and Color Lab will use the Cyber look.

**Known issues**

The blurry, upside-down image is gone.

Theme still under revision.
