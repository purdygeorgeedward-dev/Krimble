**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is free open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.38 BETA**

- **The Type tool, finished.** Tap text you already put down and the same window opens with its words, font, size, style, alignment and color in it. Change what you want and press OK (one undo step). The window also has a **live preview**: your words in the font, size, bold, italic, alignment and color you pick, changing as you pick.

- **Five fonts come with Krimble:** ROBO, Cartoonist Kooky, Cyberspace, Emoticons and Emoticons Outline, by Purdy Design. They appear in the Type tool's font list. Free for non-commercial use; commercial use needs a license from Purdy Design (purdy.design). They are not covered by Krimble's open source license.

- **Stack panels.** Long-press a panel's title bar: **Stack Below** takes a panel out of a tab group and puts it under the one it was tabbed with; **Stack Under** puts any panel under another one.

- **Panels dock when you let go right at a window edge** (within about 4 mm), at the top and bottom as well as the sides, instead of bouncing back.

- **No more double titles:** a panel in a tab group does not repeat its name in its own title bar.

- **Layers panel:** the six buttons under the list are in one row when the panel is wide enough (with smaller icons if needed), two rows when it is narrow.

- **Hex maps stay on** when you switch pictures, and Krimble remembers their size and orientation.

- **Dodge and Burn are gentle** (one drag no longer makes solid white or black) and start with a bigger brush.

- **Windows that fit the screen:** the PNG and JPG export options windows, the "Waiting for image operation to complete" window (Healing Brush, Content-Aware Fill, Scale Image, saving), and other windows that were wider than the phone.

- **JPEG files are proposed with .jpg**, not .jpeg.

- **The File > New list has bigger icons.** File > Share... is now just above Exit. The Window > Styles item (it did nothing) is gone. The vertex dots of the Polygon select tool are smaller. The About page has a line about the fonts with the purdy.design link.

**KNOWN ISSUES**

- **Free Rotate does not rotate yet.** It acts like the Transform tool and scales. Two fingers belong to the canvas zoom, so turning with two fingers cannot work; the next version turns the layer with one finger.
- The two top icons of the File > New list (Custom Document and Create from Clipboard) are a little too big.
- The Window menu can still get stuck open.
- Brushes can still show at launch.
- The toolbox has no light and dark edge lines.
- Switching to another app can restart Krimble. Krimble autosaves every 7 minutes and offers to recover your picture; if it happens a lot, set Krimble to Unrestricted in the phone's battery settings.
- Content-Aware Fill gives poor results.
- Brand new in this version and lightly tested: tap-to-edit text, the stack command, docking at the edges, the fonts, and the two-finger rotate.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
