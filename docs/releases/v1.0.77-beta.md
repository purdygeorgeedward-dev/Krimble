**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.77 BETA**

This build is 1.0.76 plus two new themes (Window > Themes). The flat theme stays the default.

**Themes**

- Krimble Cyber: see-through dark menus and panels with a cyan edge and cut corners, faint scanlines, and a background of blurred blue holographic layers over black. Titles in Orbitron, menu text in Rajdhani (both SIL Open Font License).
- Krimble Glass: frosted, see-through panels and menus with a soft mist texture, brighter top and left edges and a soft colored-light background. An open menu title is lighter glass joined to its menu with no line.
- Color Lab and the toolbox keep their own look in both themes.

**Known issues**

- From the 1.0.76 test: the first image opened shows tiny; the right panel's edge can't be grabbed; turning the phone swaps in a different panel layout; unselected panel tabs are too light; menus are cut short at the bottom; part of the Layers + button doesn't respond to taps.
