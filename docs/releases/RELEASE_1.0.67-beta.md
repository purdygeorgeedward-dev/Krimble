**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.67 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 67. The last stable release was 1.0.58 beta. You can try the more recent one, but I don't recommend it.

**Menus**

- The selected menu item is a plain blue again, with no outline.
- Menus have a black outline, so a submenu no longer blends into the menu under it.

**Layers panel**

- On a narrow panel, the top row (the three symbols, the blending mode and the filter button) splits into two rows instead of piling up.
- The + and Properties buttons have no separate arrow section over their symbols. A tap does what the button does; a press and hold opens its menu.
- The opacity bar has a margin on its left.

**Tool Options**

- "No options" (the Hand, and other tools with no options) no longer touches the left edge.

**Known issues**

- The bottom of each menu is cut off, and long names run past the right side.
- A line shows between an open menu and its name.
- The File menu sits too close to the orange K.
- A line shows on the right of the toolbox and the left of the side panel.
- The side panel's edge is hard to grab, and panels can't be torn off.
- Panels have no visible edges or inside margins.
- Krimble often restarts after switching to another app.
