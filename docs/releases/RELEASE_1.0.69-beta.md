**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.69 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 69. The last release was 1.0.68 beta.

**Painting**

- The default brush, b) Basic-5 Size Opacity, is back (it was missing since Nov 15, which gave scratchy lines).
- Smudge is the tool shown on its toolbox button, with Blur and Sharpen in its flyout.

**Image**

- Desaturate sets saturation to 0 at once, with no dialog.

**Selections, shapes and crop**

- The width and height show in the status bar while you drag, instead of in a box on the canvas.

**Menus**

- Menu text has its room above and below again.
- Krimble Painted menus have room at the bottom and right.

**Layers**

- The three symbols at the top are gone; the blending mode box fills the row.
- The top rows and the bottom buttons always keep their full height; only the layer list gets shorter.
- A tap on + always adds a layer (other layer types are in Layer > New).

**Color Lab**

- The COLOR LAB band stays at the top and never scrolls away.
- The band is as wide as the panel, so the float and close buttons follow its edge.

**Toolbox**

- The handle is taller, and a strip with a line marks the bottom of the toolbox. A long press on either opens the toolbox menu.

**Themes**

- Krimble Dark Gray uses blue selections, like the other themes.

**Known issues**

- Panels have thin margins.
- Krimble often restarts after switching to another app.
