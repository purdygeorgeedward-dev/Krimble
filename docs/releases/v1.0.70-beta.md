**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.70 BETA**

**Layers**

- The blending mode box is back at the far right of the top row, at its normal size.
- The empty space left of the blending mode box is a grab pad: drag it to tear the panel off.

**Panels**

- The empty part of every panel's title row is a grab pad with lines, sized for fingers. Drag it to move or tear off the panel.

**Under the hood**

- Old switched-off and commented-out code removed.
- The main window code is split into smaller files. Nothing changes in how the app works.

**Known issues**

- Panels have thin margins.
- Krimble often restarts after switching to another app.
