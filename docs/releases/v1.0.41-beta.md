**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, a fork of Krita, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.41 BETA**

- **Type tool: text boxes.** Drag a box with your finger and the words wrap inside it. A tap still makes a line of text where you tap.
- **Type tool: Spacing and Kern.** Spacing sets the tracking for the whole text. Put the cursor between two letters and tap Tighter or Looser to adjust that pair, with a live preview. Reset clears the kerning.
- **Type tool: font list** no longer shows the phone's Noto and Samsung system fonts.
- **Content-Aware Fill:** it tells you when it is working and when it is done, uses every core of the phone, needs much less memory, and falls back to Krita's fill instead of silently doing nothing.
- **Panels:** Krita's dotted resize handle is back on the dividers. Windows no longer bounce back after you resize them.
- **Soften** starts with a 100 px brush.
- **Transform tool:** the mode buttons are smaller.
- **View > Zoom Gadget:** the status bar's zoom slider is hidden by default so a thumb can't blow the picture up by accident. Pinch zoom still works.
- **About:** George Edward Purdy is listed first among the authors.
- **Dodge and Burn** are taken out of the toolbox for now.

**KNOWN ISSUES**

- **Smudge still turns every tool into Smudge.** After using Smudge, the Brush, Line and Circle tools keep the Smudge brush. A new fix is made for the next version.
- **Content-Aware Fill still looks like a blur.** The next version says which fill ran, and why Krita's old fill ran if it did.
- **Emoji do not show** in the Type tool.
- **File > Save:** the options window is wider than the screen.
- **Image window titles** show the title text doubled.
- **Help menu:** still lists "Benefits", a Krita item.
- **Panels:** the lock, float and close buttons are spread across the title bar instead of sitting at the right.
- **Type window:** shows a "Text" heading you may find pointless.
- **The Hand tool** can move the layer instead of the view.

Fixes for most of these are already made for the next version.

This is a beta. Please report problems on the Issues page.

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on your phone (arm64 devices).
