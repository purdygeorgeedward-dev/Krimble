**KRIMBLE**
Your Creative Companion

Krimble is a mobile-first graphics editing app for Android, with familiar industry-standard terminology, pro customizations, and touch screen oriented controls.

Krimble is open source software made with the assistance of Claude and ChatGPT.

**NEW IN 1.0.64 BETA**

**Version numbers:** this is build 64. The last stable release was 1.0.58 beta. You can try the more recent one, but I don't recommend it.

**Not tested yet.** This build holds the fixes for the faults found in build 63.

**Toolbox**

- Docked left or right, the toolbox has its own full-height column at the outer edge; docked top or bottom, its own row. The Dock menu and dragging give the same result, and a side wins over the top near a corner.
- The docked toolbox shows all its tools; it is no longer cut off halfway down.
- Heal, Smudge and Soften have names in the Retouch flyout.
- Raised style: Stamp, Gradient and UNDO have the raised box like the other buttons.

**Panels**

- A docked panel has a highlight line down its left side.
- Color Lab no longer grows past the phone's portrait width; in a wider panel it stays at the left.

**Menus**

- Menus of the menu bar fit under the bar, so every entry of a tall menu (the Window menu in landscape) is on the screen.
- The gap between the K and the menus is right from the start.

**Preferences**

- The selected page has its highlight under the icon; the icon keeps its own colors.

**Known issues**

- The Panels submenu of the Window menu was unresponsive in build 60; its cause is not found.
- The right dock vanished once when the Layers tab was tapped (build 62); not reproduced.

Found in testing this build:

- Jolts while painting.
- The image window buttons sat too high.
- With no image open, the K was too close to File.
- The Polygon and Star tool had no icon.
- Themes: raised buttons were missing in the light theme; the tab color was wrong; the selection gradient also showed in menus.
- The menu bar should be taller.
- The Quick Adjust toolbar should be removed.
- A new canvas started with the arrow tool instead of Pan.
- The toolbox order was wrong.
- There was no usable default brush.
- The slider ball bearing should be darker with a cast shadow.
- The status bar's size display was cut off in portrait.
- File Info > General had the wrong icon.
- Toolbox flyouts opened too slowly (250 ms wanted).
- The tabs for open images were gone (floating windows had become the default).
- Crashes, even on a simple fill.
